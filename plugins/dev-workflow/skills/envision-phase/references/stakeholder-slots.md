# Stakeholder slots

Check every slot below. For each, write a named role or "none" with a reason. Missing a role usually means missing a whole group of needs, so never skip a slot.

The slots follow Ian Alexander's onion model (A Taxonomy of Stakeholders, 2005). Rename roles to fit the field; keep the slots.

### Circle 1: the thing itself and the people who run it

| Slot | Who fills it | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal operator | People who use or run the thing day to day | Who touches it most often? |
| Maintenance operator | People who keep it working: repair, update, clean, restock | Who fixes it when it breaks? |
| Operational support | People who help the operators: help desk, trainers, hosts | Who answers questions from operators? |

### Circle 2: the containing system

| Slot | Who fills it | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional beneficiary | People who benefit from its outputs without operating it | Who gains from what it produces? |
| Interfacing system | Other systems, organizations or processes it exchanges things with | What does it send to, or receive from? |
| Purchaser | The person or body who pays for or acquires it | Who signs off the money? |
| Product champion (sponsor) | The person who pushes for it to exist | Who loses face if it is cancelled? |

### Circle 3: the wider environment

| Slot | Who fills it | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negative stakeholder | People harmed, displaced or opposed; includes hostile agents (fraudsters, attackers, vandals) | Who would want this to fail, or be worse off if it works? |
| Political beneficiary | People whose standing or reputation gains from it | Who gets credit if it succeeds? |
| Financial beneficiary | People who profit from it: owners, investors, partners | Who makes money from it? |
| Regulator | Bodies that set rules it must meet: laws, licences, safety, privacy, permits | Who could fine, stop or license it? |
| Developer | People who design and build it | Who builds and changes it? |
| Consultant | Outside experts who advise | Whose advice does it depend on? |
| Supplier | People who provide parts, content, venues or services it relies on | What does it need from someone else to work? |

That is 13 slots plus Supplier as Alexander's optional fourteenth. Record all of them.

### Marking roles

- Mark each named role **primary** if the vision fails without them, otherwise **secondary**.
- Each primary role needs at least one nominal scenario.
- Each role marked present needs at least one need that traces to it.
- The Maintenance operator and Operational support rows are never left blank: name a role or give the reason there is none.
