# Quality checklists

### Pick the list

- **ICT or software product** (an app, a website, a device with software, a data system): use List A, ISO/IEC 25010:2023. Its scope is ICT and software products only.
- **Anything else** (an event, a service business, a physical product, content, a process): use List B, the Volere non-functional types.
- **Both** (for example, a service business with its own booking app): use List B for the whole thing and List A for the software part.

Mark every item on the chosen list as relevant, or not relevant with a reason.

### List A: ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality characteristics

| Characteristic | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Functional suitability | Does it do the complete and correct job? |
| Performance efficiency | How fast, and with what resources, under expected load? |
| Compatibility | Does it work alongside, and exchange data with, other systems? |
| Interaction capability | Can the intended users learn and use it, including users with disabilities? |
| Reliability | How often may it fail, and how fast must it recover? |
| Security | Who may see or change what, and how is misuse detected? |
| Maintainability | How easily can it be changed, tested and fixed? |
| Flexibility | How easily can it move to new environments or scale? |
| Safety | Can it harm people, property or the environment? |

Names follow the 2023 edition; check the standard if exact subcharacteristic wording matters.

### List B: Volere non-functional types (any field)

| Type | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Look and feel | What should it look, sound or feel like? |
| Usability and humanity | Who must be able to use it, with what skills, ages, languages and abilities? |
| Performance | How fast, how many, how long, how accurate? |
| Operational and environmental | Where and in what conditions does it run: weather, venue, hours, devices? |
| Maintainability and support | Who keeps it going, and how quickly can it be fixed or changed? |
| Security | What must be protected: money, data, people, property? |
| Cultural | What customs, beliefs or sensitivities must it respect? |
| Legal | Which laws, licences, contracts, standards and insurance apply? |

### Writing a relevant quality

Write each relevant quality as a six-part scenario (Bass, Clements and Kazman):

| Part | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Who or what causes the event |
| Stimulus | The event or condition |
| Environment | The conditions at the time: normal, peak, degraded, first use |
| Artifact | The part of the thing affected |
| Response | What the thing does |
| Response measure | A number, threshold or observable pass/fail condition |

A response measure without a number or a pass/fail condition is not finished. "Fast" becomes "95% of check-ins finish within 30 seconds at peak".

### Unspoken basics

People rarely mention what they take for granted; they only notice when it is missing (the Kano model's must-be qualities). For the chosen field, list at least three basics nobody asked for, such as clean toilets at an event, a receipt after payment, or a working undo.
