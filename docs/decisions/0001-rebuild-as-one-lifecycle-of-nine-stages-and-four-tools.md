# Rebuild as one lifecycle of nine stages and four tools

## Context and problem statement

The 0.x skills were made one at a time and do not form one system (rebuild plan R2).

## Considered options

1. Nine stage skills plus suite, adopt, standards and onboard; 13 skills
2. Keep the 0.x arrangement

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Nine stage skills plus suite, adopt, standards and onboard; 13 skills".

### Consequences

- Good: every skill is a stage or a tool of one lifecycle
- Bad: all eight 0.x skills are replaced across four releases
